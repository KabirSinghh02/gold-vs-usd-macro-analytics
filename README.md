# 📈 Gold vs USD Macroeconomic Analytics Dashboard

An analytical **Power BI** solution tracking historical macro trends, price correlations, and cross-asset dynamics between **Gold Spot Prices**, the **US Dollar Index (DXY)**, inflation (CPI), and central bank interest rate movements.

---

## 📌 Business Overview & Objective
Gold and the US Dollar share a fundamental inverse relationship in global financial markets. Understanding these macroeconomic drivers allows institutional investors and treasuries to hedge risks and analyze currency-hedged commodity movements effectively.

### Key Objectives:
- Analyze historical correlation metrics between Gold prices and the US Dollar Index (DXY).
- Evaluate price volatility across major global economic events and rate hike cycles.
- Deliver interactive visuals for cross-asset macro trend comparison.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Analytics
- **Inverse Correlation Trend:** Observed strong negative correlation between DXY strength and Gold price rallies during high-interest-rate environments.
- **Inflation Impact:** Real yields and inflation expectations serve as primary drivers for Gold asset allocation over long-term cycles.
- **Volatility Spikes:** Identified key historical market stress periods where Gold decoupled from traditional currency movements.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query / Excel
- **Analytics & Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- **Visuals:** Dual-axis Trend Charts, Correlation Heatmaps, Executive KPI Cards
